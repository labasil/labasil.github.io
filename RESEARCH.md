# Creative research — 29 September 2026

## Direction

**From a petal to a celebration.** Use La Basil's existing forest green and pale gold as the stable visual language. Alternate dark botanical scenes with one ivory material study. Arabic Amiri headlines carry the story; Tajawal handles practical navigation and enquiry controls. The original brand name, leaf emblem, contact information, and business services come from the supplied local preview and brand boards.

## References and principles

- [La Basil](https://labasil.com/ar-sa/) — supplied brand context; the public URL was inaccessible to the research tool, so the supplied local Arabic preview was the content authority.
- [Aman](https://www.aman.com/) — use concise scene-led introductions and separate practical actions from immersive storytelling.
- [Aman Kyoto](https://www.aman.com/resorts/aman-kyoto) — move from a natural setting to material detail and finally hospitality, with a quiet editorial pace.
- [Aesop](https://www.aesop.com/) — use precise, restrained naming and generous separation between product/story information and navigation.
- [Awwwards GSAP collection](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/gsap/) — use one progress value per pinned scene and make transitions reversible; this is a design decision informed by the interactive-work category, not copied implementation.
- [Pinterest botanical editorial search](https://www.pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=botanical%20luxury%20editorial) — attempted as requested, but unavailable to the research tool; no image, composition, or media was taken from it.
- [MotionSites.ai](https://www.motionsites.ai/) — attempted as requested, but unavailable; no claim of inspection.

These references informed hierarchy and narrative structure. No assets were taken from their websites. Stock research inspected 16 candidates; the complete record is `ASSETS.json`.

## Selected world

Ivory bouquet → graded lily opening → sunlit orchids → hands holding a bouquet → candlelit table → actual La Basil arrangement → enquiry. The rose macro candidate was rejected from the final page because its strong pink/red palette disrupted the selected ivory/green world. The lilies have a genuinely visible opening transformation. Their original yellow/blue source is deliberately desaturated and graded to green/ivory. The table clip is trimmed to its final continuous tableware shot, avoiding the earlier edit.

The single particle event uses sampled pixels from the supplied botanical emblem. It gathers into the emblem and disperses into the celebration scene. It is deterministic, reversible, and only drawn when progress changes.
